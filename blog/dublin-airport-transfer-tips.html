Dublin Airport Transfers: How to Book a Stress-Free Pickup

What to tell your driver, where to meet, and how to make early-morning flights and late arrivals painless.

14 October 2026BCH Taxi

Whether you're catching a 6am flight or landing late at night with tired kids, a pre-booked transfer takes the guesswork out of getting to and from Dublin Airport. Here's how to make it go smoothly.

When you book, tell us

  • Your flight number and time. For arrivals, it means we can plan around delays.
  • Which terminal, if you know it (Terminal 1 or Terminal 2).
  • How many passengers and bags, including buggies, golf clubs or wheelchairs. Our 8-seater has plenty of space, but it helps us plan.
  • Child seat needs, so we can confirm what's available.

Early-morning departures

For flights within Europe, aim to be at the airport about 2 hours before departure, or 3 hours for flights to the US (US pre-clearance takes extra time). Check your airline's advice too. We'll work back from that to agree a pickup time, and we run 24/7, so a 4am pickup is no problem.

Arrivals

Once you've landed and collected your bags, give us a call or WhatsApp on +353 87 033 0046 and we'll meet you as arranged. If your flight is delayed, don't worry: let us know and we'll adjust.

Why a fixed fare helps

With a fixed price agreed before you travel, there are no meter surprises in traffic. See our airport fares, or ask for a quote if your area isn't listed.

Travelling further?

We also run direct transfers from Dublin Airport to cities across Ireland, including Galway and Cork. It's often the easiest option after a long flight, especially with luggage or a group.
