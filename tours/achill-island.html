Achill Island Private Tour

Ireland's largest island: blue-flag beaches, towering cliffs, a deserted Famine village and the Atlantic Drive.

Full dayPrivate tourUp to 8 passengersFrom Dublin: about 3 hrs 45 mins

About this tour

Achill is joined to the mainland by a short bridge but feels a world away. We follow the Atlantic Drive, drop down to the horseshoe beach at Keem Bay, walk among the stone cottages of the deserted village at Slievemore and stop in the Georgian town of Westport.

Highlights

  • Keem Bay
  • Atlantic Drive
  • Slievemore Deserted Village
  • Croaghaun Cliffs
  • Westport
  • Croagh Patrick views

What's included

  • Private vehicle for your group only
  • Up to 8 passengers, one price for the vehicle
  • Bigger group? We can arrange extra vehicles
  • Pickup and drop-off anywhere in Dublin or Meath
  • Wheelchair-accessible van with ramp
  • Flexible stops and pace on the day
